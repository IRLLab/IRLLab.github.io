01 / About us

The story has just begun. The questions are already many.

We are a young research team in the Department of Information Management, College of Public Administration, Huazhong Agricultural University, studying how artificial intelligence enters organizations, makes risk visible, and helps firms and supply chains build resilience.

02 / Research map

Turning questions into research

We study both how firms use emerging technologies and how those technologies reshape organizations and supply chains. We begin with practical management problems and search large collections of policy and corporate text for researchable signals.

IRLResearch hub
01LLM × Supply Chain

LLMs for Supply Chains

We enable language models to understand supply-chain contexts and convert fragmented unstructured information into verifiable knowledge and decision support.

  • Risk-event and actor-relation extraction
  • Domain knowledge enhancement and reliability
  • Explainable human–AI decision support
Large language models · Text mining · Knowledge graphs
02AI × Resilience

AI and Supply Chain Resilience

We explain how firms assimilate AI, improve information processing and coordination, and convert technology investments into enduring resilience capabilities.

  • AI assimilation and integration
  • Resource orchestration and supply-chain coordination
  • Response, recovery, and organizational learning
Survey research · Econometrics · Case studies
03Food Policy × NLP

Food-Security Policy Text Mining

We use accumulated policy documents, government reports, and plans to identify policy themes, instruments, coordination, and long-term evolution.

  • Historical evolution and shifting priorities
  • Central–local policy comparison
  • Policy instruments, themes, and semantic relations
  • Policy coordination and consistency
  • Links to food production, circulation, and reserves
Natural language processing · Policy measurement · Comparative analysis
04Text × Management

Policy and Corporate Text Analytics

We extract management phenomena from annual reports, announcements, news, and sustainability reports—turning interpretation into measurable, comparable, and testable evidence.

  • Strategy extraction and digitalization measures
  • Risk disclosure and organizational sensing
  • Policy coordination and firm behavior
  • Text, performance, and market response
  • NLP-based measurement of management phenomena
Text analytics · Machine learning · Causal inference

05 / Grow together

Research is not a single-player game

The lab is not only a place to complete projects and publish papers. It is also where members learn methods, develop scholarly judgment, and gradually understand the kind of researcher they want to become.

What we value

01

Problem awareness

Observe reality, identify gaps in the literature, and judge whether a question is truly worth studying.

02

Academic integrity

Data, writing, citations, and research processes should withstand scrutiny from the first source collected.

03

Independent thinking

Develop your own judgment and question existing views when evidence supports it.

04

Collaboration

Share literature, methods, code checks, and results—and help when someone is temporarily stuck.

05

Long-term growth

Outputs matter, but so do reading, expression, research, cooperation, and patience under uncertainty.

How we train

Build research capability from questions to communication
